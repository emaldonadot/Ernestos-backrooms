# Playtest Checklist

A structured checklist for playtesting The Endless Rooms, not a report generated for you — fill this in yourself (or have a tester fill it in) after each session, so multiple sessions produce comparable notes instead of one-off impressions. Copy this file per session (e.g. `PLAYTEST_2026-08-12.md`) rather than overwriting it, so you can compare across sessions over time.

## First five minutes

- What was your very first reaction on spawning in?
- Did you understand what to do without being told (interact prompt, movement, objective)?
- Did anything feel confusing, broken, or out of place in the first few rooms?

## Noticing things without being told

Answer honestly — "yes, but only after being told" and "no, never noticed it" are both useful data, not failures.

- **The Atrium (landmark room):** Did you notice it was different from every other room? What specifically stood out — scale, the mezzanine, the escalator, something else?
- **The secret room (Maintenance Sub-Office):** Did you notice the disguised bookcase door without being told where to look? If yes, what drew your attention to it? If no, would you have kept exploring long enough to eventually find it?
- **Field notes:** How many did you find and read? Did reading them make you curious about anything, or did they feel like background noise you skipped past?
- **Room variety (Storage / Office Cluster):** Did these register as meaningfully different from a standard room, or did they blend together?

## The Attendant

- Across the whole playthrough, did the difficulty feel fair — not so easy it wasn't tense, not so hard it felt unbeatable or unfair?
- Did you ever feel like you were caught through no fault of your own (e.g. spawned into it, no way to have seen it coming)?
- Did hiding spots feel useful, or did you never bother using them?
- Did you notice the Attendant reacting to noise or opened doors specifically, or did its behavior feel random/scripted to you?
- If captured: did the capture/respawn flow feel fair, or did it lose you meaningful progress?

## Environment feel

- Did the flickering lights / ambient hum add to the atmosphere, or were they distracting/annoying?
- Did anything visually break immersion (missing textures, obviously placeholder geometry, objects clipping through walls)?

## Open-ended notes

Anything not covered above — bugs, ideas, things that surprised you (good or bad), moments that stuck with you after stopping.
